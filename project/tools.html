Fitness Tools and Calculators

Understanding your body and nutritional needs can help you make informed fitness decisions. Use our fitness calculators to estimate your protein intake, body fat percentage, and BMI.

These calculators provide general estimates. Your individual needs can vary depending on your activity level, health, and fitness goals.

Daily Protein Intake Calculator

Protein supports muscle repair and recovery. This calculator uses a high-protein target of 1 gram per pound of body weight, a common fitness approach for resistance training. It is not a requirement for everyone.

Protein provides approximately 4 calories per gram. The calorie input lets you see how much of your daily energy target would come from protein.

Body Fat Percentage Estimator

Body fat percentage is an estimate of how much of your total body weight is fat tissue. This calculator uses a traditional U.S. Navy circumference-based formula.

Use a measuring tape and enter your height, waist, and neck measurements in inches. The female formula also requires hip size. Select the formula that matches the measurement method you want to use.

Measurement Tip: Keep the tape level and measure without pulling it too tightly. For the male formula, measure the abdomen at the navel. For the female formula, measure the natural waist and widest hips. Results are estimates, not exact measurements or medical diagnoses.

Body Mass Index (BMI) Calculator

BMI compares your body weight to your height. It is commonly used as a screening measurement for adults, but it does not directly measure body fat or distinguish muscle from fat.

BMI is only one indicator of health. It can be less informative for people with greater muscle mass. Adult BMI categories should not be applied to children.
